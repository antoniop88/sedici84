Tailoring production, from small runs to large-scale manufacturing. Sedici 84 unites the tradition of Italian tailoring with scalable production capacity, making Made in Italy clothing for small series, medium runs and large volumes.

We work as a contract manufacturer and in private label, supporting brands, companies and fashion houses from pattern-making through to the finished garment. Our experience allows us to maintain sartorial care, quality of materials and attention to finishing even on substantial productions.

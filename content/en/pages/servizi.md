Sedici 84 combines the tradition of Italian tailoring with flexible, scalable production capacity, creating Made in Italy garments from capsule collections to large-scale production.

We collaborate with brands, companies and fashion houses, including on private-label projects, supporting them from pattern-making through to the finished garment. Our experience allows us to maintain sartorial care, quality of materials and attention to finishing even on substantial productions.

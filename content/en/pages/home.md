Tailoring production, from small runs to large-scale manufacturing. Sedici 84 unites the **tradition of Italian tailoring** with scalable production capacity, making Made in Italy clothing for small series, medium runs and large volumes.

We work in **contract manufacturing and private label**, supporting brands, companies and fashion houses from pattern-making through to the finished garment. Our experience allows us to maintain sartorial care, quality of materials and attention to finishing even on substantial productions.

### In brief

- **Scalable production**  
  From small runs to large-scale manufacturing.
- **Contract manufacturing and private label**  
  From pattern-making to the finished garment.
- **Made in Italy sartorial care**  
  Selected materials and meticulous finishes, at every volume.
